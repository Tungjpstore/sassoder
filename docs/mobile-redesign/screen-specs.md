# Mobile Screen Specs

## Overview

First screen answers: revenue, open orders, active tables, waiting payments, low stock, and next AI recommendation. Use stacked cards and one primary CTA: `Xử lý đơn`.

## Orders

Use status tabs, channel chips, urgent-first sorting, and order cards. Card fields: bill id, time, table/channel, main items, SLA, order status, payment status, and visible actions.

## Kitchen

Use high-contrast queue cards grouped by pending, cooking, and overdue. Timer and item names must dominate the card. Modifiers and notes are highlighted.

## Tables & QR

Use a dense but tappable grid. Status colors: available, serving, reserved, dirty/maintenance, awaiting payment, overdue. Tapping opens a bottom sheet with bill, QR, payment, merge/split, and print actions.

## Payments

VietQR states: waiting transfer, transferred, waiting confirm, confirmed, mismatch, expired. Amount is the largest text in each payment card.

## Menu

Render menu items as image cards with category chips, price, availability toggle, tags, topping count, and quick edit. Desktop table stays desktop-only.

## Inventory

Low-stock alerts sit at top. Ingredient cards support quick stock update, import/export, and usage trend snapshot.

## Staff

Employee cards show attendance, role, shift, and requests. Primary actions: assign shift, approve leave, message, call.

## Analytics

Snapshot first: KPI cards, sparklines, peak hours, top items, and short insight blocks. Avoid dense BI charts on mobile.

## Reservations

Timeline/list hybrid with time, guest, party size, deposit status, table allocation, no-show risk, and confirmation action.

## AI Assistant

Floating contextual entry plus quick prompts: best sellers today, longest-waiting table, low stock, revenue reason. AI actions require clear confirmation.

## Settings

Use grouped cards for general info, payments, integrations, devices, roles, language, and account. Destructive actions are visually separated.
